We reply within 48 hours

Questions, bugs or billing issues? Email franco@varelta.com with your device model and a short description.

My apps are blocked and I want them back

Win minutes back in a game, wait for your daily reset at midnight, or remove BETLOCK's Screen Time access in Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access.

Is real money involved?

No. You only wager minutes of your own screen time. Minutes cannot be bought, sold or cashed out.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → BETLOCK → Cancel Subscription. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore purchases

Open the paywall and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in to the same Apple ID.

Delete my account

Profile tab → Delete Account, or email us and we will delete it within 30 days.
