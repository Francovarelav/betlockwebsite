How Betlock works

  • Locked by default. The apps and categories you pick stay locked until you unlock them in Betlock.
  • Daily balance. You get 240 minutes a day by default. Your balance resets every day; a negative balance carries over to the next day, a positive one does not accumulate.
  • Unlock games. To open your apps, stake minutes on an unlock game:
    • Mines — pick tiles and avoid the mines; each safe tile raises the multiplier. Cash out whenever you want.
    • Crash — the multiplier climbs; cash out before it crashes.
    • Coin flip — a 50/50 call.
  • Win and your apps open for the minutes you cashed out (stake × multiplier), from a minimum of 15 minutes up to a maximum of 4 hours. Lose and the staked minutes are gone and your apps stay locked. You can play again while you have balance.
  • Countdown. While your apps are open, a countdown shows in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen. When time is up, the apps lock again automatically.
  • Lock now. Done early? Tap "Lock now" to re-lock your apps and get your unused whole minutes back.
  • Editing your list. Adding apps to the locked list takes effect immediately. Removing apps takes effect tomorrow.

Minutes are virtual: they have no monetary value and cannot be bought, sold or cashed out. No real money is ever wagered.

Troubleshooting

My apps aren't locking

Make sure Betlock has Screen Time access: Settings › Screen Time › Apps with Screen Time Access › Betlock should be on. If you turned it off, open Betlock and grant access again, then check your locked-app list.

"Open Betlock" on the lock screen does nothing

That button sends you a notification; tap the notification to open Betlock. Turn on notifications in Settings › Notifications › Betlock.

I don't see the countdown

Enable Live Activities: Settings › Betlock › Live Activities. Your apps still re-lock on time even if the countdown is hidden.

I want my apps back without playing

You can always remove Betlock's Screen Time access in Settings › Screen Time › Apps with Screen Time Access, or delete the app. Betlock can't prevent that — it's your call.

Restore purchases

Open the paywall and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in to the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

Cancel my subscription

Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Betlock › Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel it. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com — refunds are handled by Apple.

Delete my account

In the app: Profile › Delete Account. This deletes your account data. You can also email us.
