Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what the BETLOCK app ("the App"), operated by Varelta, collects and why. We do not sell your data and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data we collect

  • Contact info — name, email address and/or phone number, depending on how you sign in (Apple, Google or phone).
  • Identifiers — an account user ID.
  • Purchases — subscription status and history, processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We never see your payment card.
  • Usage data — in-app activity such as minute balance, game results, streaks, goals and onboarding answers.
  • Diagnostics — basic crash and performance data.

All of this is linked to your account and used only to provide the App's functionality.

Screen Time data stays on your device

The apps and websites you choose to restrict, and your device usage, are handled by Apple's Screen Time framework. This information is tokenized by iOS, never leaves your device and is never sent to us.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database and multiplayer game state.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Apple — payments and Sign in with Apple.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and associated data from the App's Profile tab or by emailing us; we delete it within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or a copy of your data, or object to its processing (including rights under Mexico's LFPDPPP, the GDPR and the CCPA where applicable) by contacting us.

Age

The App is for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the date above.
