Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the iOS app "Betlock: Bet your screen time" ("Betlock" or "the App") collects, why, and what choices you have. In short: we collect only what the App needs to work, we do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and the apps you choose to lock never leave your device.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller (responsable) is Franco Varela Villegas, doing business as Varelta, Monterrey, Nuevo León, Mexico. Contact: franco@varelta.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data — your name, and your phone number and/or email address depending on how you sign in (phone SMS code, Google or Sign in with Apple), plus an account user ID. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive Apple's relay address.
  • App data — your goals and onboarding answers (including the daily screen time you report yourself), your streak, your minute balance and your bet and game history.
  • Subscription status — whether Betlock Pro is active, which product you have and when it expires.
  • Website waitlist — if you join the waitlist at betlockapp.com, your email address, the date you joined, a record of your consent (that you are 18+, accepted the Terms and this Policy, and agreed to receive launch emails), and the waitlist perk you were offered. To limit abuse we briefly keep a one-way hash of your IP address and a random session ID, which are deleted automatically within a day. We use your email only to send waitlist and launch emails, through our email provider. Every email has an unsubscribe link; you can also email us to be removed. We keep waitlist data until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, or until the waitlist ends. The website itself uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data, and we never receive your payment card details.

3. Data that stays on your device

  • Your locked apps. The apps and categories you select are handled by Apple's Screen Time frameworks as opaque tokens. They never leave your device and we cannot see which apps you chose.
  • Device usage. We do not receive your Screen Time usage data.
  • Notifications. Reminders and session alerts are scheduled locally on your device.

4. Why we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To run the App: keep your balance, streak, goals and game history, and sync them across sessions.
  • To provide and verify your Betlock Pro subscription.
  • To respond to support requests, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we do not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Where laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on: performance of our contract with you (running the App), our legitimate interests (security and fraud prevention), compliance with legal obligations, and your consent where required (for example, Screen Time and notification permissions, which you can withdraw in iOS Settings).

5. No selling, no ads, no tracking

We do not sell or rent your personal data, we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and the App contains no advertising or third-party tracking SDKs. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

6. Service providers

We share data only with providers that process it on our behalf to run the App:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) — authentication (including sending SMS codes) and our database (Cloud Firestore), where your account and app data are stored.
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) — subscription management. RevenueCat receives your account user ID and your App Store purchase receipts and transaction data.
  • Apple — App Store payments, Sign in with Apple and the Screen Time frameworks.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy terms. We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect our rights, users or the public, or to a successor if the App is reorganised or sold (subject to this Policy).

7. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries outside Mexico or your country. Where required, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards offered by the providers (such as standard contractual clauses). Transfers to providers that process data on our behalf do not require your consent under Mexican law.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the App (Profile > Delete Account), which deletes your account data stored in our database. You can also email us to request deletion. Purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat are kept under their own policies and as needed for tax, accounting or legal purposes; you can ask us to request deletion of the RevenueCat data linked to your user ID. Residual copies in backups are removed in the normal course. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription — cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

9. Security

We use the security measures provided by our providers, including encrypted connections and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights in Mexico (ARCO rights)

Under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP), you have the right to Access your data, Rectify it, Cancel (delete) it and Oppose its processing, as well as to revoke your consent and limit the use or disclosure of your data. To exercise them, email franco@varelta.com with your name, the phone number or email linked to your account, a clear description of your request, and any document that helps us verify your identity. We will respond within the time limits set by the law (generally 20 business days) and apply any approved request within 15 business days after responding. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may contact the competent Mexican data protection authority.

11. Other privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK under the GDPR or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, to know what we collect and share, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data (we do neither). You can exercise these rights by emailing us, and you will not be discriminated against for doing so. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Age

The App is only for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. We will post the new version on this page and update the date above, and for material changes we will give notice in the App.
